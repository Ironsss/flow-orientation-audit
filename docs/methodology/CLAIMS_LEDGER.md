## Erratum (2026-09-29): H2 real-data conditions

The frozen truncation levels were the fractions 1/60, 5/60, 10/60 and 1 of the window. `src/restriction/operators.py::truncate` computes each flow's offset within its window from epoch seconds (whole seconds) and keeps offsets <= window x fraction. With 10 s windows, 1/60 and 5/60 (about 0.17 s and 0.83 s) both keep only flows starting in the first second, 10/60 (about 1.67 s) keeps the first two seconds, and the fraction 1 is the unrestricted data, identical to 1:1 sampling. Two of the frozen levels were therefore not implemented as frozen, and the 7 real-data rows that clear the gates are **5 distinct conditions** (unrestricted = 1:1, 1:10, 1:100, first 1 s, first 2 s). Per-flow is significantly better in 4 of the 5 (CI of the difference below zero); topology is ahead only at 1:100 (+0.048, CI [+0.022, +0.074]), below the 0.05 preregistered margin. **The verdict (H2 REJECTED) does not change**; the number of independent conditions supporting it does. Wording such as "7/7 conditions" or "6 of 7" in earlier documents (including `docs/datos-reales/ANALISIS-DATOS-REALES.tex`, not edited because it is a dated source document) should be read as 5 distinct conditions and 4 of 5. The operator was not modified and the analysis was not rerun. The deviation was identified after the verdict was computed.
